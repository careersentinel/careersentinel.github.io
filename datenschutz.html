2. The short version

  • This site sets no cookies. Visits are counted with cookieless, privacy-preserving analytics (no cross-site tracking, no profiles).
  • We only receive personal data if you submit the waitlist form — and we use it for exactly one thing: contacting you about Career Sentinel and preparing your matches.
  • Your answers are never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • You can withdraw and request deletion at any time with one email.

3. Hosting (Vercel, Railway) and web analytics

This website is hosted on Vercel, a service of Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. When you visit the site, Vercel automatically processes technical data (in particular your IP address, browser type, date and time of the request) in server log files. This is technically necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its stability and security. Transfers to the USA are safeguarded by the EU-approved Standard Contractual Clauses in Vercel's data processing terms. Details: Vercel Privacy Policy. The site's source code is stored on GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA), which serves a legacy copy of this site that redirects here.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It is cookieless: no cookies are set, no advertising identifiers are used, and visitors are not tracked across sites. Aggregated statistics (page views, country-level region, device type) help us understand how the site is used. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in measuring and improving this website without intruding on your privacy).

The Career Sentinel application at app.careersentinel.de runs on Railway (Railway Corporation, 548 Market St PMB 68956, San Francisco, California 94104, USA). The machines and the database holding application data are located in Railway’s EU West region in Amsterdam, Netherlands, so that data is stored in the EU. Railway is a US company and its staff can in principle reach this infrastructure from outside the EU for support and maintenance. To the extent that happens, it is a third-country transfer under Art. 44 ff. GDPR, safeguarded by the EU-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two, controller to processor) in Railway’s data processing addendum. Railway is certified under SOC 2 Type II. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for application data and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for server logs. Details: Railway Privacy Policy.

4. Waitlist form

If you join the waitlist, we process the data you enter into the form:

  • Email address (required)
  • Optionally: your German level, other languages, the roles you are looking for, your visa/residence situation, and your city or region

Purpose: managing the waitlist, contacting you when your beta spot opens, and — if you provided profile details — preparing your job matches from day one. Nothing else.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, given by submitting the form) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps at your request). You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

Where your answers go: the form posts to our own application at app.careersentinel.de and your answers are stored in our own database, on servers in the EU (see section 3). No third-party form service is involved and your data is not transferred outside the EU.

What changed: until 23 October 2026 this form was handled by Formspree, Inc. in the USA. Formspree publishes no data processing agreement and we could not confirm any transfer safeguard, so we moved the form to our own servers and brought the earlier signups across. If you joined the waitlist before that date, your data did pass through Formspree at the time; it is now held only by us, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Retention: we keep waitlist data until the beta launches and you either become a user or decline — and at the latest, we delete it when the waitlist is closed, or immediately upon your withdrawal.

5. The Career Sentinel application (app.careersentinel.de)

The product itself runs at app.careersentinel.de and has its own, more detailed privacy policy. That policy is the authoritative description of how the application handles your data: Datenschutzerklärung der Anwendung. Besides Railway as the hosting provider, it uses the following processors:

  • Anthropic PBC (USA) reads your CV, scores job postings against your profile and writes your tailored CV and cover letter. CV content and your profile details are transferred to servers in the USA. Safeguard: Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two, controller to processor) in Anthropic’s data processing addendum. Data sent through the API is not used to train models.
  • Brevo (Brevo SAS / Sendinblue GmbH, EU) delivers the daily report and login emails, processing your email address and the message content. Brevo rewrites links for click tracking, so it can see which jobs you opened. No transfer outside the EU.
  • Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC) receives the daily report only if you connect it yourself. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, consent, withdrawable at any time in Settings or by sending /stop to the bot.

6. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, embeds no social media plugins and carries no advertising. The only measurement is the cookieless Vercel Web Analytics described in section 3: it counts visits, it does not identify you, and it does not follow you to other sites. All web fonts are self-hosted on this site's own server, so your browser makes no connection to Google or any other font provider.

7. Your rights

Regarding your personal data, you have the right to:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR) — ask what data we hold about you
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — have incorrect data corrected
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — have your data deleted
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR) — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) — at any time, with effect for the future

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in section 1. We will respond within the statutory deadlines.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg (LfDI), Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the service changes — for example when the beta launches. The current version is always available on this page.
